## 4. 📸 How Does Bybit Facial Recognition Work?

Bybit's Standard verification includes facial recognition. You can complete it directly via laptop camera, or scan a QR code on the desktop page to continue on your mobile phone.

### 💡 Tips for Success

- Keep your full face centered in the frame
- Ensure even lighting — no backlighting
- Clean your phone or computer camera lens
- Don't wear masks or cover facial features
- Follow on-screen prompts (turn head, etc.)

If facial recognition won't start after multiple tries, it may not be a camera issue. Bybit notes this can also happen if the previously submitted ID is non-compliant, or if too many attempts were made in a short time. In this case, **wait 30 minutes** before retrying.

> ⚠️ **Don't keep refreshing repeatedly.** If you've already failed several times, review your ID photo and facial environment first, then wait. Repeatedly submitting the same problematic data won't speed things up.
