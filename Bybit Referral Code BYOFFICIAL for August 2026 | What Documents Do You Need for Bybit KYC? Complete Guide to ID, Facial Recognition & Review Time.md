## 6. 👤 How to Pass Bybit Facial Recognition More Easily

Facial recognition confirms that the person operating the account matches the document holder.

✅ **Even lighting** — No backlighting, and don't have only one side of your face brightly lit.
✅ **Full face visible** — Center your entire face in the frame.
✅ **Remove obstructions** — Take off hats, masks, and accessories that may interfere with recognition.
✅ **Follow the prompts** — Don't shake your phone around rapidly.

If the camera keeps getting stuck loading:

- Check if Bybit has camera permissions.
- Restart the App.
- Switch to a stable network.
- Clean the camera lens.
- Avoid beauty filters or system-level filters.

⚠️ **Don't submit repeatedly a dozen times in a short period.**
If the system already shows a failure, check the reason first, then retake the photo. Excessive retries in a row may trigger additional risk controls or temporary restrictions.
