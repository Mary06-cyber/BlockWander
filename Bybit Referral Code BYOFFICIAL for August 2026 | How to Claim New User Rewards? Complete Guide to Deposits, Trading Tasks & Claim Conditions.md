Bybit's products and new user campaigns vary by jurisdiction.

### 8. Forgot to manually claim
Task showing "Completed" doesn't necessarily mean the reward has been "Claimed."

### 9. Passed the claim deadline
Some rewards have an independent Claim Deadline after task completion — missing it may void the reward.
