### I. What Exactly is Binance C2C? Understand in 3 Minutes

#### How is it different from "buying with a credit card"?

Binance C2C (also called P2P) is a platform where users directly trade crypto assets like USDT, BTC, and ETH using fiat currency. Binance acts as the **escrow**, holding the seller's USDT until payment is confirmed.

The key difference from buying with a credit card: the fiat payment happens **between you and the merchant**, not directly to Binance. This means you must learn to pick the right merchant and verify payment details.

#### Transaction Flow at a Glance
> You select a USDT seller on Binance
> 
> ↓
> 
> Create C2C order (seller's USDT is frozen by platform)
> 
> ↓
> 
> Pay to the bank account shown on the order page
> 
> ↓
> 
> Click "I've Paid" to notify the seller
> 
> ↓
> 
> Seller confirms receipt → USDT is released to your Binance account

### III. Don't Just Chase the Lowest Price! 7 Filter Criteria That Actually Matter

#### How to read completion rate, transaction count, and limits?

| Filter | How to Check | Why It Matters |
|:---|:---|:---|
| USDT Quote | Compare across merchants | Abnormally low price = red flag |
| Completion Rate | Prioritize 95%+ with long history | Reflects historical order reliability |
| Transaction Count | 500+ orders is a good baseline | Avoid pretty percentages with tiny samples |
| Single Order Limit | Confirm your amount fits | Over limit = can't place order |
| Payment Method | Pick what you actually have | Avoid scrambling for a payment tool mid-order |
| Payment Time Limit | Make sure you can finish in time | Timeout = auto-cancel + potential penalty |
| Merchant Terms | Always expand and read | May restrict banks, require specific notes |

Common traps: "Only accepts specific banks," "No transfers with numbers in remarks," "Must use instant transfer." Missing these before paying turns them into dispute triggers.

> 💡 **My take:** Prioritize completion rate and volume over price. Saving 0.5% with a merchant who has 10 trades is worse than paying slightly more to a merchant with 5,000+ trades.

### V. 4 Payment Minefields 90% of Beginners Step On

#### Minefield 1: Using a friend's bank account

Name mismatch = merchant can refuse release. Refunds can take days or weeks.

#### Minefield 2: Rounding the amount

Order says 15,238.50. You send 15,240. Reconciliation fails → dispute → delay.

#### Minefield 3: Writing "USDT" or "Binance" in bank remarks

Banks may flag or freeze transactions with crypto keywords. Leave remarks blank or follow order instructions only.

#### Minefield 4: Accepting off-order payment requests

If the merchant asks you to pay a different account, add Telegram, or pay a "verification fee" — **refuse and appeal immediately.**

### X. Why C2C Prices Differ From Market Rates (Don't Get Played)

C2C prices are set by merchants, not Binance. Influencing factors:

- Fiat supply & demand (high USDT demand → premium)
- Payment channel costs (card > bank transfer > e-wallet)
- Merchant's funding cost & profit margin
- Local USDT scarcity

**What actually matters: Total fiat paid ÷ USDT received.** Don't just look at the listed unit price.
