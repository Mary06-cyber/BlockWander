## V. Where Do Binance KYC Users Most Commonly Get Stuck?

KYC issues usually arise not because the process is too difficult, but because the information quality does not meet requirements.

### 1. Inconsistent Name or Identity Information

Your name, date of birth, and document number should match your document exactly.

Do not abbreviate your English name on your own, and do not randomly switch between different spelling methods.

### 2. Blurry Document Photos

| Check Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Document corners | Fully visible, do not crop |
| Text | Name, number, and date must be clear |
| Lighting | Avoid glare and shadow obstruction |
| Expiration date | Document must still be valid |
| Image editing | Do not use filters, retouching, or photos of a screen |

### 3. Facial Recognition Keeps Failing

Find a location with normal lighting, wipe the camera lens clean, and follow the on-screen prompts to complete facial recognition.

Hats, sunglasses, masks, overly dark environments, or your face going outside the recognition frame will all increase the chance of failure.
