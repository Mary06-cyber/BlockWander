## 6. ✍️ What Is a Wallet Signature?

One of the most common beginner concerns is seeing a Signature Request.

The key is to understand that these actions are different:

**Connect ≠ Sign Message ≠ Token Approval ≠ Blockchain Transaction**

| Action | Main Purpose | Usually Needs Gas? | Can Move Assets? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connect Wallet | Connect wallet to DApp | ❌ | Usually no |
| Sign Message | Verify wallet ownership / login | ❌ | Normally does not transfer assets |
| Token Approval | Give a smart contract spending permission | ✅ Sometimes | Creates asset permissions |
| Deposit / Swap / Trade Transaction | Execute on-chain action | ✅ | ✅ Possibly |
| Withdraw | Move assets out | ✅ | ✅ |

A normal login signature usually does not transfer your assets.

But you should still read what you are signing.

## 7. ⚠️ When Should You Stop and Reject a Signature?

If you only want to log in but see one of the following, do not confirm blindly.

### 1. The website asks for your seed phrase
A legitimate DApp connection does not require your seed phrase.

### 2. The site asks for your private key

### 3. You see an unlimited token approval you do not understand
Unlimited approval gives a contract broad token spending permission.

It is not automatically malicious, but you should know which contract is receiving that permission.

### 4. The domain is not app.nado.xyz
Close the page.

### 5. The wallet request does not match what you were trying to do
For example:

You click "Log In," but the wallet asks you to send ETH or transfer USDT.

Cancel the request and verify the site.
