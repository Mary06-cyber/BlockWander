### Step 3: Fill in Personal Information
Input the following exactly as they appear on your ID:
- **Full Name**
- **Date of Birth**
- **Residential Address**

*Note: Accuracy is critical. Any mismatch will cause rejection.*

### Step 4: Submit Identity Documents
Follow the on-screen instructions to capture or upload your ID.
**Critical Tips:**
- **Clarity:** Ensure the image is sharp and focused.
- **No Glare:** Avoid reflections on the card surface (turn off overhead lights).
- **Full View:** All four corners of the ID must be visible.
- **Authenticity:** Use original photos only. Do not use screenshots, edited images, or photos of photos.

### Step 5: Complete Facial Recognition (Liveness Check)
After submitting your ID, you must perform a liveness check.
**Best Practices:**
- **Lighting:** Face a light source. Avoid backlighting (e.g., standing in front of a window).
- **Clear View:** Remove masks, hats, or anything obscuring your face.
- **Follow Instructions:** Slowly turn your head or blink as prompted by the system.

## ❌ Common Reasons for KYC Failure

### 1. Unclear Document Photos
**Issues:** Blurry images, glare, or unreadable text.
**Solution:** Retake the photo in a well-lit environment with a steady hand.

### 2. Data Inconsistency
**Example:** Registered name "Wang Da Ming" vs. ID name "Wang Da-Ming."
**Impact:** This mismatch leads to automatic rejection.
**Solution:** Ensure the spelling and format match your ID exactly.

### 3. Poor Liveness Environment
**Issues:** Low light, dirty camera lens, unstable network.
**Solution:** Move to a brighter area, clean your lens, and switch to a stable Wi-Fi or 5G connection.
