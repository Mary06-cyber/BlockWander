## 10. 🧾 Bonus vs Coupon vs Fee Saver

**Trading Bonus**  
Usually used for eligible trading purposes and may offset certain fees or losses. The bonus principal is generally not the same as normal cash USDT.

**Fee Saver**  
Used to reduce eligible trading fees and normally comes with product and expiry restrictions.

**Coupon**  
A campaign or product-specific benefit that must be used according to the coupon's own rules.

If you see "100 USDT Bonus," do not automatically interpret that as 100 USDT that can be withdrawn immediately to another wallet or bank account.
